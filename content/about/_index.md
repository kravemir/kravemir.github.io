## About

Love open-source. Building [ArchiveKeep](https://github.com/archivekeep/archivekeep) — it helps you keep your data safe across multiple storages, without lock-in.

Believe technology should serve people, not the other way around. Tools should let you own your things — your data, your files, your freedom — instead of locking you into a company that becomes your single point of failure.

Not fond of technologies locked to individual giants. Companies can change rules anytime, whether you like it or not — and with vendor lock-in, they become a single point of abuse: forced terms-of-service changes and anti-features creep into systems you can't escape.

Stepped away from the career track for reasons that might be best left unsaid, or maybe shared someday. Now manual labor and open-source in free time.
